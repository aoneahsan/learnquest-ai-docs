# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is LearnQuest AI free?

**Yes. LearnQuest AI is free to use with no paid tiers and no subscriptions.** The learning tools run
on your device or use free, no-key public APIs, so there are no usage fees.

## What ages is it for?

LearnQuest AI is designed for **children ages 1–15**. Activities are tagged by age band so you can
match content to the right level.

## What platforms does it run on?

It runs as a **web app** (an installable Progressive Web App) and on **Android** via Google Play. The
same features are available on both.

## Does it work offline?

Many activities and tools work **offline**, and your progress syncs when you reconnect. See
[Offline, languages & themes](/features/offline-and-themes).

## Does the app really use AI?

"AI" is part of the brand name. The tools work using **on-device logic and free public APIs** (such as
Open-Meteo for weather, MyMemory for translation, and Datamuse for rhymes) plus the browser's built-in
speech. There is no paid large-language-model service behind them — which is part of why the app is
free. See the note in the [introduction](/intro).

## What does "777 activities" mean?

That figure reflects activities created by a **procedural generator** from reusable templates — the
engine combines variables to produce many age-appropriate variations. It is not 777 separate
hand-built screens. See [Activities](/features/activities).

## Do I need an account?

You can explore without signing in, but a **free account** saves your scores, badges, streaks, and
preferences across devices.

## How do parents get involved?

Parents create a parent account, **link a child**, set controls, and follow progress from the
[parent portal](/features/parents).

## Can teachers use it in class?

Yes. Teachers can **create classes, assign activities**, and review student progress from the
[teacher dashboard](/features/teachers).

## How is my data handled, and can I delete my account?

The app stores accounts and progress to sync across devices, keeps preferences on the device, and
offers **account deletion** from the profile and legal pages. See [Privacy & child safety](/privacy-and-safety)
and the in-app Privacy Policy.
