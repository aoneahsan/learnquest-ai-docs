# Privacy & Child Safety

**LearnQuest AI is built for children, so privacy and safety are first-class concerns.** This page
summarises how the app approaches data and child protection. The authoritative, always-current
details live in the app's own legal pages.

## Where the legal documents live

The app includes dedicated pages you should read for the full, current terms:

- **Privacy Policy**
- **Terms of Service**
- **Cookie Policy**
- **Data Security**
- **Account Deletion**

Open these from the app's footer or **Profile → Security & Privacy**. They are the source of truth;
this page is a friendly overview.

## Content is age-appropriate

All activities and tools are designed for **ages 1–15**. Content is kept suitable for children, and
the experience focuses on learning rather than open interaction with strangers.

## Parents stay in control

- A **parent account** can link and oversee a child's account via the [parent portal](/features/parents).
- Parents can set age-appropriate controls.
- Social features centre on learning and progress, not open chat.

## Data and accounts

- Accounts and learning data are stored to sync progress across devices.
- Preferences (like theme and sound) are saved on the device and, when signed in, synced.
- **Account deletion** is available from the profile and the dedicated deletion page, so you can
  remove your account and associated data.

## Permissions

LearnQuest AI requests only what it needs to function. Some tools use device or browser features:

- The **spelling bee** and **phonics** tools use the browser's built-in speech (text-to-speech).
- The **weather** tool calls a free public API by city name (no location permission required).

If any feature requests a sensitive permission, it does so for a clear reason and the app's privacy
policy explains it. Always refer to the in-app Privacy Policy for the definitive list.

## Honest framing

LearnQuest AI does not sell a paid "AI" service behind the scenes. The learning tools run on the
device or use free public APIs, and the app is free to use. That keeps the data footprint small and
the experience transparent.
