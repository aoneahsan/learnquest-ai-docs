# How to track progress

**This guide shows how to see what a learner has accomplished in LearnQuest AI** — from a single
score to a full picture across activities, badges, and time.

## For a learner

1. Open the **Progress** page from the main menu.
2. Review your completed activities, scores, and time spent.
3. Check the **badge showcase** for what you've earned.
4. See your current **streak** to keep your daily habit going.

Every completed activity updates these automatically — there's nothing to log by hand.

## For a parent

1. Open the [parent portal](/features/parents).
2. Select a linked child.
3. View that child's activities, scores, streaks, and badges in one place.

This lets you follow each child's learning without needing to sit with them.

## For a teacher

1. Open the [teacher dashboard](/features/teachers).
2. Choose a class, then a student.
3. Open the **student detail** view to see assigned work and results.

Use this during homework check-ins or parent conferences.

## What gets tracked

| Signal | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Score** | How well an activity was completed. |
| **Time spent** | How long a learner engaged with an activity. |
| **Badges** | Milestones and achievements earned. |
| **Streak** | Consecutive days of learning. |

## Tips

- Encourage a **short daily session** to grow the streak — consistency beats cramming.
- Use [learning paths](/features/learning-paths) so progress moves toward a clear goal.
- Celebrate badges — visible wins keep motivation high.
