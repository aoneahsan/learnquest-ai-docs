# Create an account

**An account lets LearnQuest AI save your progress, badges, and preferences across devices.** You can
explore many activities without signing in, but creating a free account keeps everything in sync.

## Ways to sign in

| Method | How it works |
|--------|--------------|
| **Google** | One tap with a Google account. On the web this uses the Firebase Google sign-in popup; on Android it uses the native Google sign-in flow. |
| **Email & password** | Register with an email address and a password you choose. |

There are no paid tiers — every account is free.

## Choosing a role

LearnQuest AI supports three kinds of profiles:

1. **Learner (child)** — the default. Picks an age band so activities are matched to the right level.
2. **Parent** — links one or more child accounts, sets controls, and follows progress from the
   [parent portal](/features/parents).
3. **Teacher** — creates classes and assigns activities from the [teacher dashboard](/features/teachers).

## Step by step

1. Open the [web app](https://learnqai.aoneahsan.com) or the Android app.
2. Tap **Sign up** (or **Sign in** if you already have an account).
3. Choose **Continue with Google** or enter an email and password.
4. Select your role and, for a learner, choose the child's age band.
5. You're in — head to the [Quick Start](/getting-started/quick-start).

## Managing your profile

From the **Profile** page you can update your display name, avatar, and age band, manage preferences
(such as sound effects), customise the theme, and review security and privacy options. Account
deletion is available from the profile and legal pages so you stay in control of your data.

:::tip Kids' safety first
For younger children, a parent should create the account and use the [parent portal](/features/parents)
to link the child. See [Privacy & child safety](/privacy-and-safety) for how data is handled.
:::
