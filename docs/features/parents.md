# For Parents

**The parent portal gives you one place to set up your child, guide what they do, and follow their
learning.** It's designed so a parent can stay involved without standing over a child's shoulder.

## Setting up

1. Create a **parent** account (see [Create an account](/getting-started/create-account)).
2. Open the parent dashboard.
3. **Add a child** or **link** an existing child account.
4. Choose age-appropriate settings and controls.

## What parents can do

| Capability | What it means |
|------------|---------------|
| **Link children** | Connect one or more child accounts to your parent account. |
| **Set controls** | Configure age-appropriate settings for each child. |
| **Follow progress** | See activities completed, scores, streaks, and badges. |
| **Stay informed** | Use the dashboard as a single window into your child's learning. |

## A typical week

- On Monday you link your two children and pick their age bands.
- Through the week each child completes activities; you check the dashboard for their progress.
- On the weekend you suggest a [learning path](/features/learning-paths) for a skill they're working on.

## Privacy

LearnQuest AI is built for children, so privacy matters. The parent portal is part of how the app
keeps adults in control of a child's experience. Read [Privacy & child safety](/privacy-and-safety)
for details on data handling, permissions, and account deletion.
