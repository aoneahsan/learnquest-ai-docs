# Offline, Languages & Themes

**LearnQuest AI is built to work anywhere, in any language, and to look the way each learner prefers.**
This page covers its offline support, internationalisation, and theming.

## Offline support (PWA)

LearnQuest AI is an installable **Progressive Web App**. On the web your browser may offer an
**Install** option; on Android you can install it from Google Play. Once installed:

- Many activities and tools run **offline**.
- Your progress is stored on the device and **syncs** when a connection returns.
- An offline indicator lets you know when you're working without a connection.

This makes the app dependable on slow or intermittent networks — learning doesn't stop when the
internet does.

## Languages

LearnQuest AI supports **multiple languages** through internationalisation, with automatic detection
of your browser's language and a language picker to switch manually. All interface text is translated
rather than hard-coded, so the app can grow to more languages over time.

## Theme customizer

A full **theme customizer** lets every learner — signed in or not — personalise the look of the app:

| Option | Choices |
|--------|---------|
| **Appearance** | Light, dark, or follow the system. |
| **Accent color** | A set of brand colours. |
| **Border radius** | From sharp to fully rounded. |
| **UI scaling** | Make everything a little smaller or larger. |
| **Font size** | Comfortable reading sizes. |
| **Panel background** | Solid or translucent surfaces. |

Preferences are saved on the device and, when signed in, synced across devices so the app always looks
the way you left it. Dark mode and adjustable text size also support comfortable reading and
accessibility.
