# Social features

**LearnQuest AI's social features let learners connect, share progress, and learn together in a
child-appropriate way.** They are designed to encourage encouragement and collaboration, not pressure.

## Friends

Add **friends** to keep a small circle of fellow learners. A friends list makes it easy to cheer each
other on and compare progress in a friendly way.

## Activity feed

The **feed** shows recent learning moments — achievements and milestones from you and the people you're
connected with. It keeps the experience lively and social without being a public, open network.

## Teams

**Teams** group learners who want to work toward shared goals. A team has its own view where members
can see collective progress. Teams pair naturally with [gamification](/features/gamification) — a team
can rally around a streak or a leaderboard together.

## Safety by design

Social features are built for an audience that includes young children:

- The experience centres on **learning**, not open chat with strangers.
- Parents can oversee a child's account through the [parent portal](/features/parents).
- See [Privacy & child safety](/privacy-and-safety) for how data is handled and what controls exist.

If you prefer, you can use LearnQuest AI purely for solo learning and skip the social features
entirely — they enhance the experience but are never required.
