# Activities

**An activity in LearnQuest AI is a self-contained, interactive learning exercise that a child plays
to practise a skill.** Activities are the heart of the app — short, focused, and rewarding, with
instant feedback and a score recorded when finished.

## Activity types

Each activity has a type that determines how it plays. The app currently supports these types:

| Type | What it is |
|------|------------|
| **Quiz** | Multiple-choice questions with scoring and instant right/wrong feedback. |
| **Puzzle** | Drag-and-drop pieces (such as a jigsaw) into the correct positions. |
| **Memory game** | Flip cards to find matching pairs and train recall. |
| **Coloring / drawing** | A canvas with colours and brushes for free creative play. |
| **Story** | A multi-page reader with optional read-aloud narration. |
| **Coding challenge** | Small programming tasks checked against test cases. |
| **Interactive game** | General interactive mini-games. |
| **Math problem** | Step-by-step math solving. |
| **Video** | A dedicated video player with chapters and progress tracking. |

When an activity references a type the app doesn't recognise, it shows a clear "activity type not
supported" message instead of a blank screen — so the experience never silently breaks.

## How activities are organised

Activities are tagged by **age band** (covering ages 1–15) and **subject**, so the **Browse** page can
filter them to the right level. Search and filters help you find exactly what you want, and a fuzzy
search makes it quick to locate an activity by name.

## Use cases

- **A 4-year-old** practises letters and shapes with coloring and simple memory games.
- **An 8-year-old** builds arithmetic fluency with math-problem activities and quizzes.
- **A 12-year-old** reads a story with comprehension follow-ups or tackles a beginner coding challenge.
- **A parent** picks age-appropriate activities and reviews scores afterward.

## About the "777 activities" figure

The app markets a large activity count ("777 activities"). To be transparent: that number reflects
activities produced by a **procedural content generator** built on reusable templates — the engine
combines variables (letters, numbers, colours, animals, and so on) to create many activity variations
across age bands. It is not 777 individually hand-authored screens. The generator and templates live
in the app's data layer, and the result is a broad, varied library that stays consistent and
age-appropriate.

## Completing an activity

When you finish an activity, LearnQuest AI records your **score** and **time spent**, updates your
[progress](/guides/track-progress), and may award a [badge](/features/gamification). Many activities
work offline and sync your results when you reconnect.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an account to play activities?**
You can explore activities without signing in, but a free account saves your scores, badges, and
streaks across devices.

**Are activities safe for young children?**
Yes — content is designed for ages 1–15. See [Privacy & child safety](/privacy-and-safety).

**Can activities be played offline?**
Many can. LearnQuest AI is offline-ready and syncs progress when a connection returns. See
[Offline, languages & themes](/features/offline-and-themes).
