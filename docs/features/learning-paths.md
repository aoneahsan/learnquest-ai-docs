# Learning Paths

**A learning path is a guided sequence of activities that builds a single skill step by step.** Instead
of choosing activities at random, a learner follows a path from start to finish, always knowing what
comes next.

## How a path works

1. **Pick a path** from the learning-paths list that matches a goal (a subject or skill).
2. **Work through the steps** in order — each step is an activity or short lesson.
3. **Watch your progress** fill in as you complete each step.
4. **Finish** the path and carry the new skill into other activities.

## Why use a path instead of browsing

| Browsing activities | Following a path |
|---------------------|------------------|
| Great for exploration and variety. | Great for steady, structured progress toward one goal. |
| You choose what to do next. | The path chooses a sensible order for you. |
| Progress is spread across many topics. | Progress is focused and easy to see. |

## Use cases

- A learner who wants to **get better at multiplication** follows a math path that builds from basics
  to harder problems.
- A child learning to **read** moves through phonics, simple words, and short stories in order.
- A parent or teacher points a learner at a path so they always have a clear "what's next."

Open a learning path from the app's **Learning Paths** section, then use the detail view to start the
next step. Your progress is saved automatically and contributes to your overall
[progress tracking](/guides/track-progress).
